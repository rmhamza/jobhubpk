Questions

Do you apply to jobs for me?

No. You review each job and click Submit yourself on the employer's page. That keeps your accounts safe and your applications better.

Which sites do you search?

We search the open web and company career pages. We never open LinkedIn, Indeed or Glassdoor pages. If their links appear in results, you open them yourself in your own browser.

How does payment work?

Send the monthly amount by JazzCash or Easypaisa, then upload the screenshot when you sign up. We check it and activate your account.

What happens when my month ends?

Your access stops. Sign up again with the same email and password and send a new payment to renew.

Is my resume private?

Yes. It is stored only in your account, used only for your matches and letters, and you can download or delete it any time.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Contact us and we will change your plan.
